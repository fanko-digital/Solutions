# FanKO AI Digital Solutions

FanKO AI Digital Solutions is an AI-powered digital marketing agency web application rewritten in React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS on Vite.

## Features Preserved & Ported
- **AI-Powered Dark Cyber Theme**: Retains signature Orbitron and Space Grotesk typography, cyan/neon glow effects, and interactive ambient canvas particle background.
- **Multi-View SPA Routing**: Instant, zero-reload switching between Home, About Us, Services, Portfolio, Blog, Blog Article Detail, and Contact views with URL browser history sync.
- **Interactive Counters & Metrics**: Animated count-up numbers for clients served (150+), satisfaction (98%), campaigns run (500+), and years of experience (5+).
- **Comprehensive Marketing Services**: Interactive cards for all 10 core offerings including SEO, Google Ads, Web Development, Mobile App Dev, Email Marketing, Google Business Profile (Local 3-Pack), Social Media Marketing, Meta Ads, AI Video Commercials, and Branding.
- **Portfolio & Case Studies**: Detailed client success stories for WheelWarriors (Kanyakumari), Sana Hindi Academy (Erode), Don Crackers & Traders (Sivakasi), and Grand Festive Cafe GFC (Kanyakumari) with ROI and growth metrics.
- **Interactive Testimonials Carousel**: Rotating client reviews with auto-slide, previous/next controls, and active indicator dots.
- **Blog & Guide Hub**: Live keyword search and category filtering (All, SEO Tips, Local SEO, Digital Marketing, AI Marketing, Website Dev), plus full article view for the 2026 Kanyakumari Digital Marketing Guide.
- **Interactive Contact & WhatsApp Integration**: Validated consultation form that builds formatted WhatsApp messages and opens direct chat, along with direct phone, email, and Google Maps embed.
- **Persistent Floating WhatsApp CTA**: Floating action button for instant chat from any page.

## Tech Stack
- **Framework**: React 19 SPA
- **Tooling**: Vite 6, TypeScript
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS, custom design tokens, and CSS animations
